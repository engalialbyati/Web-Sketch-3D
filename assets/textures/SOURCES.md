# Bundled texture sources

Seven seamless colour maps from **ambientCG** (ambientcg.com), dedicated to
the public domain under **CC0 1.0** — no attribution required; this file
records provenance (the asset IDs resolve at
`https://ambientcg.com/list?id=<ID>`):

| File | ambientCG ID | Default real-world tile |
|---|---|---|
| `wood_bark.png` | Bark014 | 0.5 m |
| `wood_bark_olive.png` | Bark012 | 0.5 m |
| `stone_rock.png` | Rock058 | 1.5 m |
| `stone_moss_wall.png` | Rock064 | 1.5 m |
| `stone_river_pebbles.png` | Rocks022 | 0.4 m |
| `grass_lawn.png` | Grass005 | 0.6 m |
| `paving_concrete.png` | PavingStones128 | 0.5 m |

The 512×512 downscalings were made by the IngeTrazo project; downscaling a
CC0 work stays CC0, and the ambientCG originals remain available as
seamless PBR sets at up to 8K.

Not copied from any third-party project, on purpose: IngeTrazo's generated
textures and water are GPL-3.0 (its own original work), its 397 Sweet Home
3D textures carry that library's terms, its face-me people are the author's
personal artwork, and its `.igz` components mix CC-BY and Free-Art-License
models in a format this app does not read. The RAL Classic table this app
ships (`js/ral-colors.js`) comes from Dezzpil/RAL-JSON (**MIT**).
